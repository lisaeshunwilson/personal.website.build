Scientific and technical consulting

I hold a PhD in Molecular Cell Biology from UC Berkeley and spent a decade in structural biology research, including a postdoc in Integrative Computational and Structural Biology at Scripps Research and a position as a scientist at an AI-first protein design company. Consulting engagements draw on that work directly.

Capabilities

  • Cryo-electron microscopy: experimental design, data processing, structure interpretation
  • Structural and biophysical characterization of protein complexes
  • Machine learning approaches to protein design and protein structure prediction
  • Scientific review of computational biology methods and results
  • Technical writing and grant preparation for life sciences

MindShift

MindShift is an AI-guided wellness platform that matches users to structured yoga, breathwork, and meditation sessions based on their reported needs.

The design question behind it is whether personalized delivery improves adherence to mind-body practice, which is the point at which most digital wellness products fail. Development is ongoing.

app.yogawithlisa.ai/mindshift
